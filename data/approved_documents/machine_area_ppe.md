# Approved Safety Manual: PPE Near Heavy Machines

Employees and visitors entering heavy machine areas must follow posted plant requirements and supervisor instructions.

## Common PPE

- Safety helmet or hard hat where overhead or struck-by hazards are present.
- Safety glasses with side shields in production and maintenance areas.
- Face shield when grinding, cutting, or handling splash hazards, used in addition to safety glasses.
- Hearing protection in high-noise areas or where signs require it.
- Safety shoes with toe protection and slip-resistant soles.
- Cut-resistant gloves when handling sharp parts, sheet metal, or tooling. Gloves must not be worn near rotating equipment when the SOP prohibits glove use.
- High-visibility vest or clothing in forklift, crane, or mobile equipment zones.
- Respirator only when required by the task assessment and issued under the plant respiratory protection program.

## Safe Conduct Around Machines

Stay outside marked hazard zones unless assigned and authorized. Do not reach into moving equipment. Do not bypass guards, interlocks, light curtains, or emergency stop devices. Report missing guards, damaged PPE, spills, abnormal noise, or unsafe conditions to the supervisor.

## Important Boundary

PPE reduces risk but does not make a task automatically safe. Supervisors and safety officers decide task requirements based on the machine, process, and site rules.
