# Approved SOP: Lockout-Tagout Basic Explanation

Lockout-tagout, often called LOTO, is a safety process used before cleaning, servicing, adjusting, or repairing equipment. Its purpose is to prevent unexpected startup or release of stored energy.

## Standard Steps

1. Notify affected employees that the equipment will be shut down for service.
2. Identify all energy sources, including electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, thermal, gravity, and stored mechanical energy.
3. Shut down the equipment using the normal stop procedure.
4. Isolate each energy source using approved isolation points.
5. Apply a personal lock and tag to each isolation point according to the site procedure.
6. Release or restrain stored energy, such as pressure, elevated parts, springs, or residual electrical charge.
7. Verify zero energy state using the approved verification method before beginning service.
8. Keep locks and tags in place until the work is complete and the area is clear.
9. Remove locks and tags only by the authorized person who applied them, unless the site has a formal exception procedure.

## Explanation

LOTO is like putting the equipment into a controlled "cannot start" condition. The lock physically prevents energy from being restored. The tag explains who applied the lock and why. Verification is required because turning a switch off does not always remove stored energy.

## Important Boundary

Only trained and authorized employees may perform lockout-tagout. This document explains the process but does not authorize any person to perform work.
